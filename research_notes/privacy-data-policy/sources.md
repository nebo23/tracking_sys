Status: design research, not legal advice. Nothing here establishes legal compliance, certification, provider program approval, or app-review access. Qualified counsel should confirm jurisdictional positions before the owner records Q-003 or Q-012. Market candidates are listed neutrally; no market is implied by inclusion.

## Web access limitations
- EUR-Lex returned empty pages. GDPR article text comes from the gdpr-info.eu reproduction of Regulation (EU) 2016/679; ePrivacy Art. 5(3) wording comes from EDPB quotations. Verify against EUR-Lex before citing in contracts or notices.
- ICO pages returned HTTP 403; ICO points rest on search excerpts and commentary. The CURIA docket could not be read.
- Some EDPB, NIST, DIFC, ADGM, and Egyptian translation PDFs could not be parsed by WebFetch. Their text was extracted locally with already-installed tools in the session scratchpad; nothing was installed. One research pass could not extract EDPB 2/2023 and 01/2025 and relied on summaries.
- SDAIA rejected requests for official Saudi texts; Saudi points rely on law-firm summaries. The CPPA regulations PDF returned 404, California leginfo returned 403, and several law-firm article pages returned 403 or 307.
- developer.x.com returned HTTP 402; the X Developer Agreement and Policy were read from docs.x.com. The current Facebook Login deauthorize guide returned 404. Many developer pages show no last-updated date.

Confidence: high means a primary or official text was read; medium means official text was partial or a reputable secondary source was used; low means conflicting or weak sources.

## Gaps
- Official EUR-Lex GDPR and ePrivacy text, ICO guidance pages, and the CURIA docket were not read directly. No final EDPB pseudonymization guidance was found; EDPB Guidelines 02/2026 on anonymization (consultation) were not read in full.
- Not researched: EU Member State ePrivacy exemptions (for example CNIL audience measurement, German TDDDG section 25), Art. 35 DPIA thresholds, Art. 26 joint-controller arrangements, children's data, special-category data, and email or SMS marketing rules for captured leads.
- Saudi official texts, the outcome of the Saudi localization draft, Egyptian official Arabic decree text, PDPC adequacy lists or cookie guidance, UAE federal executive regulations after August 2026, ADGM current deadlines, Qatar and Bahrain request deadlines, Jordan and Morocco adequacy lists, and Kuwait instrument numbering remain unverified. Oman and other markets were not covered.
- Official CCPA regulation text was not retrieved; whether server-side conversion APIs constitute "sharing" has no regulator-specific source; no multi-state US matrix was compiled.
- Provider gaps: current Facebook Login deauthorize documentation; YouTube scope sensitivity classification and the text of the 2026 derived-metrics policy; LinkedIn Conversions API email normalization; Google Ads Customer Match and Data Manager API; TikTok and other providers; CRM, scheduler, and form-tool policies; Paymob PCI attestation; official PCI DSS v4.0.1 text.
- Technical gaps: NIST SP 800-57 cryptoperiods and SP 800-88 cryptographic-erase conditions were not read, so no cryptoperiod is proposed; privileges for PostgreSQL control-data functions are untested; production KMS, journal storage, and export storage were not researched.
- No pattern was prototyped or performance-tested. Retention durations, backup retention, RPO/RTO, and tombstone duration remain owner decisions.
