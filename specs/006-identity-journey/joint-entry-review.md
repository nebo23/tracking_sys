Status: approved. D-075 (2026-11-23) approves J1-J12 below as recommended, without amendment, and authorizes phases P0-P5. Preparation was performed under D-074.

## Intended result

An authorized agency member can follow a client's consented, evidence-backed customer journey and connect a verified sale to that journey. A refund changes money; an identity correction changes who is associated with the sale. Neither operation silently changes the other. A sale remains valid financial evidence when its purchaser is unknown or has been erased.

The work is reviewed together but implemented in ordered, independently verifiable slices. This combines 006-A to 006-E and 007-B/C; it does not implement persisted attribution runs, 016-B reports, a React interface or live provider onboarding.

## Decisions for the owner

The recommendations are for synthetic data and disposable PostgreSQL. They are product and data-policy choices, not assertions of legal compliance. Existing production questions remain open.

| ID | Recommended choice | Alternative and practical effect |
|---|---|---|
| J1 Scope and delivery | One branch from `81977a3`, with the phases below; 006-A to 006-E and 007-B/C handoff verification are included. Publish verified commits under D-051 and present the completed scope for independent review. PR creation and merge remain separate. | Separate branches and reviews: smaller deliveries but another integration/reconciliation step between identity and finance. |
| J2 Identity evidence | Adopt 006 D2, D4, D5 and D9: trusted client-backend assertions only; verification at most 30 days old and 5 minutes ahead; email, international ASCII phone and client account reference; client-scoped keyed identifiers only, with no stored raw contact values or display masks. Shared/conflicting keys stay ambiguous; a phone cannot pull a new account into an existing person. No automatic merging of existing people. | Accept unverified form values or keep contact values: greater matching/display coverage, but a new trust/storage policy and substantially more sensitive data. |
| J3 Consent and proof | Adopt 006 D3, D6 and D7: a 10-minute single-use browser handoff bound to a backend challenge; direct proof only for server-issued subjects with their current identity grant. Legacy issuance is `unknown` and cannot link. Link retrospectively only inside the uninterrupted identity-consent period. Withdrawal removes identity associations (W1); expiry stops new association and preserves eligible prior assignments. | Link legacy subjects or retain withdrawn associations: more history, but the former cannot establish issuance and the latter needs a different approved privacy basis. |
| J4 Access | Adopt 006 D8: Owner/Admin manage identity; Analyst/ClientViewer need explicit per-client `can_view_identities`, default false. Read service keys have no identity access in v1. Person-linked financial amounts and financial-derived customer qualification require both identity and finance read access. Financial writes/reconciliation remain Owner/Admin only; ingest keys may assert identity and sale association but cannot change amounts. | Add a dedicated finance-writer role or give all client readers identity access: a broader permission design and grant migration. |
| J5 Corrections and stages | Adopt 006 D10/D13: audited reversible merges of disjoint pairs, optimistic versions, immutable original ownership and a change feed. Trusted lead evidence establishes a lead. A currently associated verified capture establishes historical customer status even after a full refund; refunds remain visible financially. No financial record alone keeps an identity profile alive after its identity evidence expires. | A net-positive customer definition would remove customer status after full refunds; physical merges would make correction harder. Both change product behavior. |
| J6 Erasure and retention | Adopt 006 D12 and F1 for this development slice: erase/detach person associations while retaining non-contact financial receipts and amounts. Ambiguous shared-device events remain anonymous and keep the deletion request on hold until an Owner/Admin resolves that exact set. Identity evidence/persons: at most 395 days under the detailed rules; retry receipts: 24 hours; change feed: 30 days; settled holds: 30 days after request completion; closed merges: 2 years or earlier erasure; active merge edges: while both participants exist, with no age limit. Financial records, materialized history and reconciliation actions follow the existing workspace-lifetime development policy. | Delete financial rows on person erasure (breaks reconciliation), automatically erase unresolved shared-device events (may remove another person's events), or make old merges irreversible (bounds retained correction history). |
| J7 Schema and dependencies | Approve the concrete schema inventory in the [joint design addendum](joint-design.md): the eleven identity tables plus six ledger/association tables, scoped constraints and specified existing-column/grant changes, in additive migrations `0026`-`0028` after `0025_whatsapp`. Use existing dependencies; test on disposable databases only. | Fewer derived tables with calculation on every read: less storage, but repeated expensive reconstruction and weaker durable consumer checkpoints. |
| J8 Financial persistence | Persist immutable receipts and signed movements with current transaction projections, per-client serialized receipt order, deterministic recomputation and compensating release/post movements. Preserve D-070 signed-order checks. A newly held/released input changes state at a new receipt; it never rewrites journal history. Approve the existing R007-02 release policy explicitly. | Commitment-first accounting would keep the first posted adjustment when later evidence disagrees. This changes existing 007-A behavior and requires new financial rules. |
| J9 Reconciliation | Expose the pending/conflict queue to finance readers and audited, idempotent review actions to Owner/Admin. An action acknowledges or reopens an investigation; it cannot accept contradictory provider evidence, forge a parent, erase a receipt or mark a manual declaration verified. Existing manual/CSV entries stay unverified. New verified correction intake is outside this packet. | Permit staff to override provider evidence: requires a separate evidence standard, approval roles and financial correction protocol. |
| J10 Sale association | Accept a separate trusted association assertion naming the client-scoped source account and sale operation plus a valid 006 identity assertion. Require current identity and attribution consent, same credential/source scope and a presently valid ownership segment. Never match on amount, time, campaign or unsourced payment ID alone. Unknown/conflicting matches remain unlinked/ambiguous. Corrections append association revisions; they do not alter financial evidence. | Import customer identifiers from Paymob/CRM automatically: requires new provider-field collection and source-specific verification policies. |
| J11 Currency and limits | Preserve exact original-currency amounts and the existing synthetic FX contract. No live FX source, default reporting currency or mixed-currency total. Keep current approved intake and report bounds; benchmark persistence separately and leave A3 open until measured write/rebuild scaling is acceptable under a later load decision. | Add live currency conversion and increase limits now: requires Q-004/Q-006 decisions, a provider, costs and measured capacity. |
| J12 Completion evidence | Verify all six deferred 006 findings, persisted AC1-AC5 and R007 regressions, concurrency, erasure/rebuild races, tracker browser journeys and compatibility with 014-016. Verify attribution recalculation through the existing in-memory 008 engine against actual persisted synthetic 006/007 inputs. Clearly retain production 008 persistence and 016-B/C as open. | Include production attribution persistence now: expands this workstream to a third specification and its decisions. |

J2-J6 retain the named original 006 recommendations except where the addendum explicitly resolves a gap or specifies customer-stage behavior. J7 replaces the obsolete migration number and expands the schema for finance; J8-J11 define the previously unreviewed 007-B/C choices.
