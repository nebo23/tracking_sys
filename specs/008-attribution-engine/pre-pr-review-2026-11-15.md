# Spec 008-A pre-PR review

**Disposition: Request changes before publication.** Reviewed local commit `3d772d1eb1449952f7a6cf924214870ccfcfe671` against merged `main` at `bc0ff40` and D-046. GitHub returned no PR for the specification branch. The findings below concern the in-memory calculation approved for 008-A. All examples used synthetic inputs and public interfaces. Financial replay examples used the actual 007-A ledger.

## Findings

Line numbers refer to [the attribution engine](../../backend/app/attribution/engine.py) at the reviewed commit. Monetary examples in prose are in major units unless marked otherwise.

| ID | Priority and line | Observed result | Regression needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| R008-01 | P1 · 681 | Register two conversion IDs pointing to the same agency, client, source account and sale, one for each of two people. A single capture of 100 credits campaign A with 100 and campaign B with another 100 under the same model. The registry enforces uniqueness only by conversion ID. | A scoped sale contributes once per model. Check retries, conflicting person associations and independent sales that reuse references in other source accounts. |
| R008-02 | P1 · 683, 724 | A settlement effective on Nov 3 arrives first. A Nov 2 campaign touch receives 100. A subsequent capture sets the same sale's authoritative effective time to Nov 1 in 007-A. The net and movement journal do not change. Incremental attribution retains the Nov 2 touch, while a fresh calculation assigns the 100 to unknown. Registering the corrected conversion raises `conversion_conflict`. | Reconcile ledger timing changes even when the signed balance stays constant. Keep the earlier version traceable and verify incremental versus fresh window, evidence and allocations. |
| R008-03 | P1 · 625, 661 | With a verified sale of 100 and refund of 20, recording the genuine ledger snapshots at positions 1, 2, then 1 publishes balances 100, 80, then 100 as the latest revision. Also, initializing a consumer with an existing history and registering its already credited sale writes a new zero-revenue revision at position 0 before replay. | Prevent an older financial position from superseding a newer run, including when reusing a populated history. Preserve same-position revisions for genuinely updated journey evidence. |
| R008-04 | P1 · 728–740 | After registering a 100 sale, deliver a 20 refund and a subsequent audited +20 correction together. The checkpoint advances from 1 to 3 with no revision. Delivering those same two receipts separately records the balance at 80 and its restoration to 100. Audit history depends on transport batching. | Distinct ledger receipts that change allocations produce traceable signed differences even within one delivery batch. Verify identical economic revisions across batch sizes. |
| R008-05 | P1 · 739 | Capture 100, refund 100, then receive a fully reversed 100 chargeback. The real ledger emits movements 3 and 4 for one receipt: −100 and +100. The final net is zero. Consuming movement 3 on its own raises `invalid_net`; delivering movement 4 next raises `movement_gap`. The consumer remains at position 2 unless the caller reconstructs the pair. | Define an atomic receipt boundary or buffering protocol. Every supported page split must converge with full replay, without publishing a negative transient balance or silently losing a movement. |
| R008-06 | P1 · 473 | Under First Click with eligible A then B, A keeps all credit after B withdraws consent. The current history still lists B as eligible. Fresh calculation correctly excludes B with `consent_not_granted`. This changes the eligibility of a formerly qualified touch. | The current view must reflect consent and ownership changes even if the winning allocation is unchanged. Preserve the historical version separately. |
| R008-07 | P2 · 478, 565 | Reassign the same touchpoint ID from campaign A to campaign C in a new authorized journey snapshot. A 100 sale moves to C, yet the new revision has empty causes and differences. A campaign report applying the signed differences would keep A's 100. | Include changed campaign/content provenance in the explanation and provide a reversible reassignment for downstream aggregation. |
| R008-08 | P2 · 718–723 | Supplying two identical copies of a single new ledger movement to one call raises `movement_gap` at checkpoint 0. In the opposite direction, an overlapping input with a different movement at an already applied position is silently treated as a retry. A 100 capture followed by a different ledger prefix with a 90 capture and a 20 refund leaves the consumer at 80 against that prefix's 70. The latter is a journal mismatch. | Coalesce identical movements, distinguish conflicts from gaps, and reject a mismatched prefix before applying its suffix. |
| R008-09 | P2 · 588–604 | Looking up a conversion using the same agency, client and conversion ID, but a different person, transaction and source account, returns the original person's run. A conflicting model definition with the same version ID also retrieves a run computed under the other model. Within the same history that version ID can mean First Click for one conversion and Last Click for another. | Validate the registered conversion and model definition on history writes and reads. Exercise repeated IDs within one client. Cross-tenant disclosure was not observed. |
| R008-10 | P2 · 731 | A valid ledger journal has signed movements of `MAX_MINOR - 1`, +1, and −1 minor units. The final transaction balance is representable. Applying the complete batch raises `invalid_amount` while adding the second movement and leaves the checkpoint at 0. | Handle exact accumulation and define the outcome for unrepresentable intermediate totals. A representable final result should agree with the ledger or return an explicit recoverable receipt-level status. |
| R008-11 | P2 · 389, 473 | Correct the winning touch from the first occurrence of 01:30 in New York on Nov 1 to the second occurrence using the same timezone object. Both instants are in the window. History reuses the previous run and reports 05:30 UTC although the corrected evidence is 06:30 UTC. | Compare evidence and effective times by absolute instant when detecting revisions, as the eligibility calculation already does. |
| R008-12 | P2 · 362–364, 579 | The public history calculation accepts a currency change for the same sale. Replacing 100.00 AAA with 10.000 BBB leaves the integer minor-unit count at 10,000 and produces an empty differences list. | Enforce the transaction's original currency, including exponent, across its revisions before computing differences. |

The window validator also accepts `timedelta.max`, for which calculating a 2026 lead raises a raw `OverflowError` at line 434. Validate or safely handle an out-of-range lower bound.
