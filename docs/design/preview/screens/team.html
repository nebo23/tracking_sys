What each role can do

Owner
Everything, including billing and removing the agency. There is always at least one.
Admin
Manages clients, connections, people and keys for every client.
Analyst
Reads the clients they are granted. Sees revenue and spend only where financial data is granted too.
Client viewer
Someone from the client's own team. Reads their one workspace and changes nothing.
Every change here is written to the audit log.
