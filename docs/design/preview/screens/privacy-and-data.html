Privacy and data

How Connect counts the visitors of every client, how long it keeps what it collects, and the erasures asked for.

Design concept. There is no agency-wide privacy page in the API yet. This page gathers rules that are already decided and the erasures each client page already shows; it needs its own decision before it is built.

How visitors are counted

Nothing before consent
A website tracker counts a visitor only after they agree, on every client’s site. This is the default; a looser rule is an open decision.
Global Privacy Control is respected
A browser that sends the signal limits what an agreement can allow.
No names, emails or phone numbers
Forms are registered field by field, and the kinds Connect never collects are refused.
