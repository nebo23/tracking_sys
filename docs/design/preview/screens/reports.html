A download is checked again each time. Erasing a person’s data, disconnecting an account or archiving the client removes the exports it concerns at once.

Who sees an export

Owners, admins and analysts see every export of this client that they may read. An export with sale values appears only to people with financial access.

A client viewer sees the exports they saved and those of the schedules they receive.

The person who saved an export, and owners and admins, can revoke it. Names of members are shown to owners and admins, who can read the team.

Saving a report

Every report page has “Save as export” next to “Download CSV”: Content, Revenue, Ad spend and the three tabs of Attribution. The export keeps the same CSV, with the same days and choices.

Reports are CSV files. Printable pages and PDF files are not part of this round.
