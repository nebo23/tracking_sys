A content marketing SaaS for agencies and their clients. The first commercial release combines content performance analytics with financial transaction tracking. Agencies can use these capabilities to evaluate content across their authorized social accounts and trace leads, verified sales, refunds, and net revenue back to content when evidence is available. The confirmed technology stack is FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, and Docker.
